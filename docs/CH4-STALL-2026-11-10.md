# ch4 (kongkeat) — the Shorts feed stopped showing the channel (2026-11-10)

## What changed

The earlier read ("watched but unengaged") was wrong. Measured with the Analytics
and Data APIs on 2026-11-10:

| window | Shorts-feed views |
|---|---|
| 11-01 .. 11-06 (6 days) | **4,340** |
| 11-07 .. 11-10 (4 days) | **6** |

Daily views collapsed on 11-06 and never recovered:

```
10-29 821   11-03 789   11-05 1534
10-30 661   11-04 440   11-06  482
11-01 406                11-07   16   11-08..10  0-3
```

Per clip (live Data API counts): clips 01-14 (posted 11-05 .. 11-06 morning) hold
8-242 views each; clips 15-51 (posted from 11-06 13:30) hold **0-2 views each** —
37 clips at ~0. The feed is not showing them.

## It is not a channel strike

`channels.list` / `videos.list`: status `public`, uploadStatus `processed`,
`longUploadsStatus allowed`, no `rejectionReason`, no `regionRestriction`,
embeddable, stats public, not made-for-kids. Nothing is blocked; reach is withheld.

## It is not the burst alone

genx posted 26 clips in a day and dharma 49, and both kept working. kongkeat posted
17-22/day. So the volume is not sufficient by itself. What kongkeat has that the
others do not: **every clip uses the same voice and the same family-story template**,
so 47 clips in three days read as one repeated thing.

## The panel (K's rule: no decision to a person)

Five advisors answered (openrouter had no key); all five agreed:

1. Repetition at that rate reads as spam / "audio signature" on a young channel →
   the feed stops showing it. (gemini-flash, gemini-preview, pollinations)
2. Stop posting for a few days to reset the signal; do not keep posting the same
   shape.
3. Do **not** delete the flat clips — a bulk delete is itself a negative signal.
4. Come back at 1-2 clips/day with changed content (new voice/template/hook).

## What the loop does now

`scripts/pace.py`:

- `detect_stall()` — the newest `STALL_LOOKBACK` (5) clips all at or under
  `SUPPRESS_VIEWS` (5) means the feed is off, not a weak hook. A clip with real
  reach clears it; a spent quota (no view data) is an unknown, never a stall.
- `gate()` runs the stall check **first**: a stalled channel is paused
  (`allowed=false`, verdict `bad` so the next shape changes). After
  `STALL_COOLDOWN_H` (20 h) it allows **one** changed clip as a probe, then re-reads
  — so the loop tests the feed daily instead of flooding it.
- `stats()` now also returns `likeCount` / `commentCount`; `gate()` reports
  `engagement_per_1k` on the last post, so a young channel is read **the same day**
  (K, 2026-11-10: hours, not days; multi-day waits are for a channel that already
  has many subscribers).

Verified live: kongkeat flagged stalled and paused; genx and dharma unaffected.
Guards: `scripts/test_pace.py` (`test_stall`, `test_gate_stall`). 29/29 tests pass.

## The deeper lever: the hook itself (measured 2026-11-10, later same day)

The feed stop was not only pace. Analytics retention, averaged over every clip with
data:

| channel | clips | opening | mid | end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| genx | 37 | 140% | 62% | 40% |
| dharma | 41 | 145% | 68% | 47% |
| **kongkeat** | 11 | **104%** | **13.7%** | **7.5%** |

Same ~45 s length on all three. The difference is the hook shape:

- kongkeat: a flat warm scene -- "บ่ายวันเสาร์พ่อจูงมือฉันไปนั่งพับนกกระดาษในสวนหลังบ้าน"
- genx/dharma: a question or a startling truth -- "เพื่อนครึ่งชีวิตไม่ได้อยากเห็นเราได้ดีกว่า"

The channel's script generator **forced** the flat shape: the prompt required "a
concrete family scene with a physical act" (a rule written when those hooks got
460-1,083 views), and its 20 curated angles were all that shape. So the pool filled
with 51 flat clips; 37 of them got the feed withheld.

A five-advisor panel (gemini x4 + pollinations; openrouter has no key) was unanimous:

1. The weak non-tension hook (13.7% mid) is the cause; the pace was the amplifier.
2. Replace the forced "concrete scene" rule with **"family context + tension"**: a
   question, a hidden truth, a loss, or a let-down inside the first 3 seconds.

### The fix

- `gen_short_scripts.py`: kongkeat angles rewritten to tension subjects; the prompt
  now demands a tension hook and gives examples; a `_TENSION` regex is enforced in
  `valid()`, so a flat draft is rejected before it is rendered.
- `publish_shorts.py`: the same rule as a local `_TENSION` (the rail never imports
  the generator) plus `require_hook` on the kongkeat spec -- the rail **holds back**
  the pool's flat leftovers instead of spending slots on the shape that got the
  channel withheld.
- `panel_script.py`: the vetting rubric for kongkeat now judges by the tension hook.
- Guard: `test_pace.test_tension_parity` (the two copies must stay identical; kongkeat
  requires it; the healthy channels do not).

Proven: the generator, asked for 6 fresh kongkeat scripts, returned 6 tension hooks
(all open with "ทำไม" / why). The rail would publish the 2 tension clips already in
the pool and hold the other 24 flat ones.
