## The pick: **INTC — Intel Corporation**

**Why (short-term, 1–3 weeks):**
- Deepest oversold in the whole universe: RSI14 29.5, and it sits -39% below its
  50-day MA and -58% off its 52-week high — the most "room to snap back".
- A reversal already started on the day the data was taken: +5.5%, the second
  strongest move in the list (behind ARM, which the budget can't buy whole).
- 1-month return -41.4% = heavily sold off, the classic mean-reversion setup for
  a short horizon.
- Fits the budget: 59.37 leaves ~40 USD cash buffer with one whole share.

**Levels (USD):**

| | level | note |
|---|---:|---|
| Entry | ~59.40 | market; no need to wait |
| Stop-loss | **54.00** | ~-9%; under the recent psychological/ swing low |
| Target | **70.00** | ~+18%; reward:risk ≈ 2:1 |
| Money management | buy 1 share (~59.37) | keep ~40 USD cash; do **not** average down |

**Single biggest risk: a falling knife.** INTC is a structurally challenged
business (foundry losses, competition from TSMC/AMD). The +5.5% day may be a
dead-cat bounce; if it loses 54.00 the thesis is wrong — cut, do not hold and
hope. There is also **gap risk** from news/guidance, which is why a real stop
must be placed in advance.

**Earnings check (measured, not assumed):** Intel last reported **2026-10-29**
(stockanalysis.com). The next report is the Q4 print in ~late January 2027, so
the 1–3 week window is **clear of an earnings gap**. (Confirm the exact date at
the broker before entering.)

## What the panel agreed on regardless of pick

1. **Scale in / small size** — every candidate has a negative `v50`, i.e. the
   short-term trend is still down. This is a trade, not an investment.
2. **Always place the stop** — several names sit close to their 52-week low, so a
   new low has no support beneath it.
3. **Do not chase the day's biggest gainers** (AMAT +11.4%, LRCX +10.4%): the
   entry after a vertical day is worse than waiting.

## Honest limits of this result

- The team **split** (INTC 3 vs SNAP 2 vs SOFI 1). INTC is the plurality and the
  only name an independent (non-Google) vendor also chose, but this is a
  shortlist-plus-a-named-risk, **not** a confident consensus.
- `us_dip_scan.py` is **price-only**: no earnings model, no valuation, no news. A
  dip can be a falling knife for reasons the scan cannot see.
- This is technical, short-horizon screening — **not investment advice**. 100 USD
  is real money; the stop is what protects it.
