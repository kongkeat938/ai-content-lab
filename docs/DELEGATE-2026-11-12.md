# Delegation — take K out of the loop for good (B, 2026-11-12)

K chose **B** (2026-11-12): *add a second account as a manager, so the team can act
forever without K*. This is the runbook. One step is K's and is done **once**; after
that the team owns every recovery.

## Why this is the right cut (measured)

Two advisor panels (fast team + OpenHands Cloud) agree the rogue uploader rides a
credential held outside these rails, and that the cut is `oauth2.googleapis.com/revoke`
plus a GCP client-secret rotation. We measured that **no API key can do the GCP side**:

| We hold | We cannot reach |
|---|---|
| GitHub Actions secrets (scopes `repo, workflow`) | GCP (`cloudresourcemanager` → 403; token scope is `auth/youtube` only) |
| `oauth2.googleapis.com/revoke` | the Google account (Google blocks this host) |
| the Data API (quota-gated) | creating an OAuth client; K's PC1 (dead) |

Google ties a grant to a **person**, not to a key. So the permanent fix is not
another key — it is a second **person/account** with manager rights, which the team
can drive. That is B.

**Manage permissions** → **Invite**), add the second account as **Manager** (or
Owner). The channel→Brand Account map:

| genx | `@tatagenxgeny` | `kongkeat.938@gmail.com` |

The three channels are on **three separate Google accounts** (K, 2026-11-12), so each

19/day upload pool. The map is locked by `scripts/test_channel_accounts.py` against

Short link: **https://myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts**

Then, on the second account, accept the invite and grant the YouTube access once
(same OAuth flow as `scripts/yt_auth.py`, signed in as the second account). That
mints the delegated token; from then on the team holds a credential K does not have
to touch.

## What the team does after (no K)

1. `python3 scripts/delegate_ready.py` — proves a delegated credential exists and
   reaches the channel (live, quota-free: it exchanges the refresh token).
2. `python3 scripts/revoke_token.py --channel <ch> --confirm` — cut any grant, then
   re-consent from the delegated account (no K).
3. `rogue-guard` + `private-delete` keep running every 30 min / daily — the rogue is
   paused within the hour and the parked set is deleted after the quota reset.

## Readiness is measured, not assumed

`scripts/delegate_ready.py` checks each channel for a **delegated** credential in
`YT_<CH>_ALT_REFRESH_TOKEN` and reports READY / NOT-DELEGATED. A channel with no
delegated credential is a real gap, and the script exits non-zero so a rail can see
it. Until the delegation is done, the containment rails still hold — the rogue cannot
stay public, it just is not cut at the source yet.

## What "done" looks like

- `delegate_ready.py` prints READY for all three channels.
- A revoke + re-consent from the delegated account restores the rails with **no K**.
- The `:21` monitor shows zero new `en-US`/category-27 clips.
