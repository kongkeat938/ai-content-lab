## Executed the strategy — real build, and it caught a shipping bug (2026-10-30)

Rebuilt ep02 end-to-end on a fresh pod (ffmpeg + edge-tts + Pillow reinstalled;
the pod had recycled). This was not a rehearsal — it found a defect that would
have shipped.

- **The concat demuxer truncates a still-image slideshow.** `make_video.py` fed
  the 13 poster JPEGs straight to `-f concat` with `duration` directives. Output:
  **648.9 s** against a **662.8 s** narration — **~14 s dropped**, so the closing
  line "ราตรีสวัสดี" (starts 658.5 s) never played. Reproduced in isolation:
  sum of `duration` = 662.832, ffmpeg output = 648.880. The trailing duplicate
  entry, `-t`, `-vsync cfr` — none of it fixes it. **Fix: encode each card to a
  clip of its own exact length (`-loop 1 -t seg`), then `-c copy` concat the
  clips.** Output 662.8 s, gap 0.0 s. Verified the tail is real: audio 656–663 s
  measures -21.4 dB mean (not silence) and the last frame at t=660 is not black.
- **Guard added:** `quality_gate.py` now has `video.matches-audio` (video vs
  audio within 1 s). It fired on the broken build (exit 1) and passed after the
  fix. General rule: **a gate that never fails is not a gate — but the failure
  must be a real one.** This one was.
- **`make_short.py`** (new, in `work/research/`) cuts a vertical 10–59 s Short
  from a finished episode on narration-line boundaries, with a burned Thai
  caption; blur-pad 16:9 → 1080x1920. ep02 hook = lines 4:9 (15.3 s).
- **`color_range=tv` needs the colorspace tags.** `-color_range tv` alone leaves
  `color_range=unknown` in the stream; `-colorspace/-color_primaries/-color_trc
  bt709` are what make ffprobe report `tv`. (Both make_video and make_short.)
- ep02 rebuilt: audio **-14.4 LUFS / TP -3.4 dBFS**, 251/251 lines matched,
  video 662.8 s, gate **0 failures / 0 warnings**. Server returns **206** for
  Range → seekable. Commit `3d21d59` on `research/competitor-strategy`.
- Packaging applied: ep02 tags grown to 12 (added #ปล่อยวาง #ข้อคิดชีวิต
  #นิทานสอนใจ #ฟังก่อนนอน); `build_api.py` regenerated site/api.
