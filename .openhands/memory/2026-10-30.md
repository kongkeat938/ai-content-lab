## What was built (all in `work/research/`, committed on branch `research/competitor-strategy`)
- `yt.py` — mints an access token from the per-channel refresh tokens, read-only
  YouTube Data API helper.
- `collect.py` → `raw.json` (149 channels, 272 videos in the 3 niches),
  `deep.py` → `recent.json` (latest 50 uploads of 13 competitors).
- `COMPETITOR-STRATEGY.md` — measured market per channel, what wins, our
  differentiators, a 90-day plan with numeric targets, and blockers.
- `RUNBOOK.md` — the repeatable per-episode loop + pre-publish checklist.
- `quality_gate.py` — checks a finished episode; exit 1 on hard failure.
- `measure.py` — our median views vs the §6 targets.
