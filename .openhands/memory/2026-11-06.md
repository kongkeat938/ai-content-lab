## 📉 Title test read at 57 min — NOT meaningful, and the real reason
- 11 title changes went live 12:27–12:29Z; read 13:25Z (**57 min**). Aggregate
  +290 views (x1.30 before/after) BUT per-video ratios 0.26–4.74 = **noise**, and
  inside the channel's normal daily swing (genx 2026-11-01=344, 11-02=588, 11-03=3130).
- **DECISIVE (Analytics `insightTrafficSourceType`, 7d):** views are **93–97% Shorts
  feed**, only **1.5–2.5% YouTube search** (genx 98.4/1.5, dharma 93.9/1.8, kongkeat
  96.9/2.5). 10 of the 11 changed videos are **Shorts (40–54 s)** → their views come
  from the feed, NOT from the title. **A Short title rewrite cannot move Shorts views.**
  The one long-form changed (`8Xiq1ganmRs`) went 0→0.
- Conclusion: don't add more Short titles. The **testable lever is long-form**
  (search is its path): genx 17 long-form, dharma 10, kongkeat 4, all at 1–36 views.
- Kept the 11 changes + `docs/views_snapshot.json` (60-video control) for a 14-day
  re-read. Detail: `docs/TITLE-TEST-2026-11-06.md`.
